Helivision

A camera-based helmet prototype that turns visual detections into directional feedback.

The challenge

Helivision explores how wearable computer vision can support partially visually impaired riders with information about nearby vehicles. It combines on-device inference with simple visual, audio and haptic feedback.

From camera to cue

  • A camera service captures frames with Picamera2 and writes a shared image for the detection process.
  • TensorFlow Lite runs object detection on the Raspberry Pi, keeping inference on the device.
  • Detection information drives the prototype’s LED indicators, OLED displays, voice output and haptic cues.

Engineering work

The work covers camera integration, an embedded inference pipeline and hardware feedback. Migrating to Picamera2 addressed camera and operating-system compatibility. The repository separates camera capture from detection so both processes can share a single stream.

Prototype scope

The repository describes a vision-only final prototype. Ultrasonic and radar sensing were explored experimentally and are not presented here as final components. The front-view illustration is a conceptual reconstruction of the documented hardware, rather than a photograph or measured assembly drawing. This is an assistive research prototype; no road-safety certification is claimed.
